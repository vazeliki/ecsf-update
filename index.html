Extended ECSF Cybersecurity profiles

This is a demonstration of how the extended ECSF will look like.

The 12 ECSF profiles have been revised and updated. This demo presents the revised tasks and their associated skill areas. By clicking on each skill area, users can see how the corresponding skill is expected to evolve across different proficiency levels.

For each skill area mapped to an ECSF profile, an expected proficiency level is also assigned. This represents the maximum level of competence expected for that particular profile. For example, the CISO profile is assigned Governance & Strategy at Level 4. This does not imply that every CISO must start at Level 4; a more junior CISO may naturally begin at Level 1 or 2 and progressively develop towards the expected Level 4.
